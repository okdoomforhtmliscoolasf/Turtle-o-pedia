01 / THE OCEAN GROUP

Seven living sea turtle species.

Sea turtles belong to the superfamily Chelonioidea. They have flippers and streamlined bodies adapted for marine life. The leatherback is the only living member of its family, Dermochelyidae.

🌊

SEA TURTLE · CHELONIIDAE

Green turtle

Chelonia mydas — named for the greenish colour of its body fat, not a green shell. It is known for grazing on seagrass as an adult.

SEA TURTLE · CHELONIIDAE

Hawksbill

Eretmochelys imbricata — recognised by its narrow, hooked beak and overlapping shell scutes. It is strongly associated with coral reef habitats.

SEA TURTLE · DERMOCHELYIDAE

Leatherback

Dermochelys coriacea — the largest living turtle. Its shell is leathery and supported by a mosaic of small bones rather than a typical hard shell.

River & pond turtles

A large variety of families inhabit rivers, lakes, wetlands and ponds. Some swim actively; others spend much of their time walking along the bottom.

Tortoises
