FIRST, THE BIG PICTURE

One order.
Many worlds.

All modern turtles belong to the order Testudines. The group includes marine turtles, freshwater turtles and tortoises — with different bodies, habitats and ways of moving.

AN ANCIENT BLUEPRINT

The shell is not a suitcase.

A turtle’s shell is part of its skeleton. The upper carapace is connected with the spine and ribs; the lower plastron forms the belly. It is not a separate house that a turtle can leave behind.
